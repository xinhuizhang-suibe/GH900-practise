# GH-900 Pre-Exam Checklist / 考前检查表

## One Week Before the Exam / 考前一周

- [ ] Confirm the latest exam scope, duration, and policies on the official certification page.<br>在官方认证页面确认最新考试范围、考试时长和规则。
- [ ] Confirm the appointment time, time zone, exam language, and delivery method.<br>确认预约时间、时区、考试语言和考试方式。
- [ ] Complete at least two rounds of practice questions.<br>完成至少两轮练习题。
- [ ] Reach at least 80% accuracy in weak domains.<br>薄弱领域正确率达到 80% 以上。
- [ ] Explain every commonly confused concept.<br>能解释所有高频易错对比。

## One Day Before the Exam / 考前一天

- [ ] Verify that the name on your identification matches your registration.<br>检查身份证件姓名与报名信息一致。
- [ ] Test your network, camera, microphone, and exam software.<br>测试网络、摄像头、麦克风和考试软件。
- [ ] Prepare the exam area and remove prohibited items.<br>整理考试环境，移除不允许的物品。
- [ ] Review only mistakes and quick-reference notes; do not learn new material.<br>只复习错题和速查表，不学习新内容。
- [ ] Get enough sleep.<br>保证充足睡眠。

## Exam Day / 考试当天

- [ ] Sign in early and complete the identity and environment checks.<br>提前登录并完成身份与环境检查。
- [ ] Identify single-answer, multiple-answer, and "best answer" questions carefully.<br>仔细识别单选、多选和“最佳答案”题。
- [ ] Eliminate clearly incorrect options first.<br>先排除明显错误选项。
- [ ] Mark uncertain questions and review them at the end.<br>标记不确定题，最后复查。
- [ ] Apply least privilege, standard collaboration workflows, and security-first principles.<br>关注最小权限、标准协作流程和安全优先原则。

## Question-Answering Principles / 做题原则

1. For permission questions, choose the minimum access required to complete the task.<br>权限题优先选择“完成任务所需的最低权限”。
2. For security questions, choose the action that immediately reduces risk, such as revoking an exposed credential.<br>安全题优先选择立即降低风险的动作，例如撤销泄露凭据。
3. For collaboration questions, first determine whether the scenario requires an Issue, Discussion, or PR.<br>协作题先判断场景需要 Issue、Discussion 还是 PR。
4. For automation questions, distinguish workflow, event, job, step, runner, and action.<br>自动化题先区分 workflow、event、job、step、runner 和 action。
5. Treat absolute wording such as "only," "all," and "always" with extra caution.<br>遇到“只能”“所有”“永远”等绝对表述时要特别审慎。
